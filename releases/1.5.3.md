## What's new since 1.5.2

### Detection

- **Rust** is reported as an app's language, next to Swift and Objective-C, when its executable embeds the Rust standard library. Apps that mix Swift and Rust show both.
- New stack: **wgpu**, for Rust apps that draw with their own GPU renderer instead of a toolkit. Iced and egui apps, which also use wgpu, are still reported as Iced and egui.
- Apps with large executables are detected much faster: matching embedded strings in a 150 MB binary took seconds, and now takes a fraction of one.
- Cached results are re-detected on first launch.
